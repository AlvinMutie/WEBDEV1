Weekly Timetable

We hold four sessions every week during the semester. Components and tools are provided at the lab, so you only need to show up.

Day Time Session Who It Is For Venue
Tuesday 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM Arduino and electronics basics New and first-year members Engineering Block, Lab 4
Thursday 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM Robot design, CAD and 3D printing Project team members Mechatronics Lab
Saturday 10:00 AM - 1:00 PM Open build and test session All members Mechatronics Lab
Sunday 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM Programming and simulation (online) All registered members Online (Google Meet)
Extra build sessions are organized in the two weeks before major competitions.

Annual Competitions and Events

The club calendar revolves around these events:

  1. JKUAT Innovation Week (March) - we demonstrate club projects to students, staff and visitors.
  2. Inter-University Robotics Challenge (June) - our main competition, with line-following and sumo categories.
  3. RoboFest Kenya (August) - a national event where we enter one senior team and one beginner team.
  4. Orientation Week Showcase (September) - live demos for new students at the club exhibition stand.
  5. Internal End-of-Year Contest (November) - a friendly competition where every team presents its best build.
